Scouting report

Why No. 5: The tools are top-3 level, but the free-throw shooting is already pushing him down.

Offense

3-and-D wing who spaces the floor.

Defense

Wing stopper because of his length.

Strengths

A 7'1" wingspan, he blocks shots from the wing, and he's very young for the class.

Weaknesses

51.4% at the line is a red flag for a shooter. Thin frame, and he floats through stretches.

Swing skill

Free throws and catch-and-shoot threes. A 51% free-throw shooter rarely turns into a 38% NBA three-point shooter.

NBA comps

CeilingTrey Murphy III

Murphy is the long shooting wing who became a big-time starter. Vassell is a long, thin shooting wing who blocks shots and makes plays on defense, which is what Branch does at 6'7" and 190. Reddish is the floor: all the length, never consistent.
