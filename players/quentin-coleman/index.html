Scouting report

Knockdown shooterOffensive rebounderAbove-rim athlete

Why No. 1: A shooter, rebounder and athlete who is already running a top-10 team's offense at 18. That production is why he's my No. 1.

Offense

Combo guard who shoots off the ball and now runs the offense.

Defense

Active guard who rebounds and gets steals.

Strengths

He shoots it (50.6% from three as a senior), finishes above the rim, and crashes the glass. He led Team USA U18 in rebounding and is averaging 4.7 assists as Illinois' lead guard.

Weaknesses

He's 6'4" and slim, and he has to prove the high-volume three holds against Big Ten length.

Swing skill

Running an offense full time. If the playmaking sticks, he holds No. 1.

NBA comps

Mitchell is the explosive 6'3" guard who rebounds, gets steals and started off the ball before taking over as a creator. Suggs is the athletic two-way guard who plays passing lanes and scores without being a full-time point guard. Grimes is the floor: an athletic 6'4" guard who shoots and defends but never became a creator.

Role and situation

Projected starter (CBS lineups) but not an on-ball player: CBS puts him behind Mirkovic, Vaaks and Stojakovic in the pecking order. Role is cutter, slasher, spot-up shooter and offensive rebounder with defensive disruption. Spent the summer adding strength (WCIA).

247 Illini: true combo guard, efficient spot-up threat off the ball. Illini Now (SI): could become the No. 1 option but a balanced attack is more likely. Locked On Illini: motor and rebounding earn minutes under Underwood.

Background

Starting-caliber off-ball SG. Knockdown shooter (50.6% from three as a senior), relentless offensive rebounder, and a plus athlete who finishes above the rim. Led Team USA U18 in rebounding. CBS (Sept 2026): 'Coleman has no off switch.' An excellent offensive rebounder who adds takeaways to a defense that usually lacks them.

HS senior (Principia): 23.1 PPG, 6.1 RPG, 3.9 APG, 2.7 SPG, 64.9 FG%, 50.6 3P%, 89.7 FT%. Summer 2026 U18 AmeriCup: 11.2 PPG, 11.4 RPG (tournament best), +114 in 5 games. 2025 EYBL (Bradley Beal Elite): 11.9/5.2/2.4, off-ball role. Iverson Classic MVP (31 pts, 12 reb).
