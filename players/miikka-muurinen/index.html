Why No. 35: His archetype is coveted. His college production so far isn't.

Offense

Pick-and-pop big who attacks closeouts.

Defense

Switches in space and helps at the rim.

Strengths

He's a 7-footer who can play away from the basket and finish above the rim. He has EuroBasket experience against grown men.

Weaknesses

The efficiency isn't there yet: 3-18 from three and 47.3 TS%. He's thin.

The three. His whole profile depends on it.
