Scouting report

Passing hubMobile bigMotor

Why No. 31: His feel and passing are bankable. His age caps the upside.

Offense

Dribble handoffs, short-roll passing, cutting.

Defense

Drops and shows on pick-and-rolls, with some switching.

Strengths

His passing is among the best of any big in the class. He moves his feet well in space.

Weaknesses

He's a senior, 22.9 on draft night. He's light for a center and has no reliable jumper.

Swing skill

Strength. Can he hold position against NBA centers?
