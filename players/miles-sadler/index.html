Why No. 28: He's one of the best scorers on the board. His size and shooting keep him in the late 20s.

Offense

Lead guard scoring off the bounce.

Defense

On-ball pressure.

Strengths

He's quick and can score at volume as a freshman. He had 18 against No. 1 Florida.

Weaknesses

He's 6'0" and 170, and 10-42 from three (23.8%) is a real problem for a small guard.

Swing skill

Pull-up three. Small guards have to shoot.
