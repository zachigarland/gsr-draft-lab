Scouting report

Downhill driverStrong framePhysical defender

Why No. 20: He's built for the NBA and fits a role, but his shooting doesn't support a lottery pick yet.

Offense

Attacks closeouts and drives through contact.

Defense

Switches 2 through 4 and guards bigger wings.

Strengths

He has an NBA body already. He finishes through contact and rebounds well for a wing.

Weaknesses

He's 3-14 from three. His handle is mostly straight-line, and he has little pull-up game.

Swing skill

Corner three. If it hits 35%, he's a starter.

NBA comps

CeilingDillon Brooks
LikelyIsaac Okoro
FloorJustin Champagnie

Okoro fits him almost exactly: a strong 6'5" slasher who defended right away and needed years to fix his jumper. Brooks is the version where the shot comes around and he defends with real edge. Champagnie is the floor: a strong-bodied wing who sticks on rebounding and effort.
