Gar's Scouting Report

An independent NBA Draft scouting project. One big board, a full report on every prospect, and free tools to mock the draft yourself.

How I rank players

  • Does it translate? Every stat gets checked against whether it carries to the NBA. Assist volume from an undersized guard who can't get downhill is the classic example of a number that doesn't.
  • Production over hype. A player doesn't hold a high spot on age, recruiting rank or consensus alone. He has to produce.
  • Outlets are a signal, not the answer. I cross-check ESPN, Bleacher Report, CBS, NBADraft.net, FanSided and others to find where the room disagrees, then make my own call.
  • Ceiling and floor both matter. Tiers reflect who a player can become, so a rotation-level ceiling gets capped no matter how big the numbers are.
  • Comps have to fit. Every prospect gets a ceiling, likely and floor comp that matches his build, role and how he scores, not just his stat line.
  • Eligibility first. Nobody goes on the 2027 board until their draft year is confirmed.

How the site stays current

Stats refresh every day from ESPN box scores. Pro and international stats come from RealGM and league sites. Every move on the board comes with a note on why, and each report lists its sources.

The draft tools

The mock draft, board builder and lottery sim all read straight from the board, so they update with it. The lottery uses the new 3-2-1 rules that start with the 2027 draft. Traded picks follow the published protections and swaps, and team needs come from current NBA player stats.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no ads. Your mocks and boards save only in your own browser.
