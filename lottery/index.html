How the 3-2-1 lottery works

  • 16 teams: the 10 that miss the playoffs and play-in, the 9 and 10 seeds, and the two teams that lose the 7 vs 8 play-in game.
  • Missed the play-in: 3 balls each (8.1% at No. 1).
  • Three worst records: "draft relegated" to 2 balls (5.4%). They cannot fall below No. 12.
  • 9 and 10 seeds: 2 balls. Losers of the 7 vs 8 game: 1 ball (2.7%).
  • All 16 picks are drawn. Everyone except the relegated teams can fall to No. 16.
  • No team's own pick can go No. 1 two years in a row or top five three years in a row. That rules Washington out of No. 1 and Utah's pick out of the top five.
  • Picks 17 to 30 go to the other 14 teams, worst record first.
